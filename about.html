Rooted In
Tradition.

Earthly Basket began with one question — why should healthy have to mean complicated? Here's how we've spent every day since answering it.

Good food should
feel like home.

We grew up on millets, malts and slow-cooked traditional meals — food that took time, and tasted like it. Earthly Basket exists to keep that flavour and nutrition intact, while giving it back the time modern life rarely allows.

EST. COIMBATORE
How We Started

A kitchen table idea.
A grandmother's recipe.

Earthly Basket started in a home kitchen in Coimbatore, recreating the millet porridges and traditional meal mixes our families grew up on — because store shelves were full of "healthy" food that tasted nothing like it. We tested every recipe on our own families first, and we still do.

How We Source

Farmers we know.
Grains we trust.

Every millet, grain and spice is sourced directly from farming communities across Tamil Nadu and Karnataka who grow the way their families always have — without shortcuts. We roast and grind in small batches, close to harvest, so nothing loses its nutrition sitting in a warehouse.

Our Journey

Our Story, Mile by Mile

From a small kitchen in Coimbatore to your homes, here's how Earthly Basket has grown over the years.

2021

The First Batch

Earthly Basket began in a home kitchen in Coimbatore, remaking family millet recipes for modern mornings.

2022

Direct From Farmers

We moved to sourcing millets and grains directly from farming communities across Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

2023

The Traditional Meals Line

Pongal, Kesaribath, Bisibele Bath and Veg Bath mixes launched, built for everyday ease while staying true to tradition.

2024

FSSAI Certified Kitchen

We moved into a licensed production space, scaling batch quality without changing the recipes.

2026

5,000+ Households

Earthly Basket now reaches homes across India, one wholesome breakfast at a time.
